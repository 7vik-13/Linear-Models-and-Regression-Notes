## Linear Models & Regression Notes

This repository contains `.tex` and `.pdf` files for notes from the course _Introduction to Linear Models and Regression_, a 4th-semester B.Math course at ISI Bangalore.

I translated these notes into LaTeX during my summer vacation, based on the source material by Moutushi Chatterji. I had originally planned to LaTeX a specific portion of the syllabus, but unfortunately, I could not complete it. I may complete the remaining notes if possible, depending on the demand.

Please note that these notes are __not exhaustive and may contain errors__. Use them with caution and refer to the original source material when necessary.
